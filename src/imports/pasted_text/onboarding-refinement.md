Refine the onboarding experience of the existing Admin Web application.

Do NOT redesign the application.

Keep the existing design system, colors, typography (Roboto), Auto Layout, components, variables and responsive behavior.

The onboarding experience should become the very first workflow after successful Login or Sign Up.

This onboarding should be inspired by enterprise SaaS products like Atlassian, Notion and Zoho People, but it MUST follow our finalized Product IA and business flow.

Do NOT copy Zoho's UI or Information Architecture.

Only use it as inspiration for the onboarding experience.

Keep spacing consistent.

Use Roboto.

Keep the interface clean and enterprise-ready.

The onboarding should feel like setting up an enterprise SaaS product rather than filling a long form.
